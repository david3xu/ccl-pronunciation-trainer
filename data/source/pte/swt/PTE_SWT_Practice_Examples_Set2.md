## Example 1: 2014 Olympics (Medium)

### Original Passage

11 February 2009 – **Major athletic events around the globe** – from the 2014 Sochi Olympics to an annual powerboat race in Norwegian fjords – **are striving to neutralize their carbon footprint as part of a world-wide climate network,** the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP) said today.

**The sporting events are the latest participants to join the network, and are particularly important for inspiring further global action on the environment,** said Achim Steiner, UNEP Executive Director.

"It is, therefore, heartening that **this passion for excellence increasingly encompasses environmental performance,** with sporting organizations raising the bar ever higher at each and every tournament," he added.

Organizers of the 2014 Sochi Olympic and Paralympic Winter Games – to be held in a unique natural setting between the shores of the Black Sea and the soaring snow-capped Caucasus Mountains – say they will put an estimated $1.75 billion into energy conservation and renewable energy.

**That investment will be dedicated to improving transport infrastructure and offsetting greenhouse gas emissions** from the use of electricity, air travel and ground transportation, the reforestation of Sochi National Park and the development of green belts in the city.

### SWT Answer

**Major athletic events worldwide are working to neutralize their carbon footprint as part of a global climate network, while sporting events are the latest participants to join and are especially crucial for encouraging global environmental action, since the passion for excellence progressively encompasses environmental performance, so the investment will concentrate on enhancing transport infrastructure and offsetting greenhouse gas emissions.**

**Word count:** 59 words ✓

### Key Changes Made

- "around the globe" → **"worldwide"** (phrase simplification)
- "striving" → **"working"** (verb change)
- "world-wide" → **"global"** (synonym)
- "particularly important" → **"especially crucial"** (adjective changes)
- "inspiring further" → **"encouraging"** (verb change, simplified)
- "action on the environment" → **"environmental action"** (reordered, kept key terms)
- "increasingly" → **"progressively"** (adverb change)
- "focus on improving" → **"concentrate on enhancing"** (verb changes)

### What I KEPT (Important!)

- ✅ "neutralize their carbon footprint" (key specific term)
- ✅ "participants" (important word from highlighted section)
- ✅ "passion for excellence" (key concept)
- ✅ "environmental performance" (key specific term)
- ✅ "transport infrastructure" (key term)
- ✅ "offsetting greenhouse gas emissions" (key specific term)

**Linking words used:** as, while, and, since, so ✓ (Varied, not repetitive!)

**AI Score:** 8.5/9 with feedback: "paraphrase some words"

**Lesson Learned:** When AI says "paraphrase some words" at 8.5/9, it means change MORE verbs and adjectives/adverbs, but DON'T over-paraphrase key specific terms like "neutralize carbon footprint", "passion for excellence", or "environmental performance". The strategy is to change verbs aggressively while keeping key concepts intact.

In the cave of Enlene, for example, many bones of reindeer and bison fetuses were found. Apparently, upper Paleolithic people hunted like other predators and killed the weakest prey first. They did, however, sometimes concentrate on salmon suns and migrating herds of reindeer. Contrary to popular beliefs about cave man, upper Paleolithic people did not live deep inside caves. **They rather closed the foot of cliffs,** especially when an overhang provided good shelter.
