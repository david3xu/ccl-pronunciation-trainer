## About Answer Short Question

Answer Short Question is part of the PTE Speaking section where test-takers must listen to a question and provide a brief, accurate answer in one or a few words. This collection contains 676 real exam questions covering various topics including science, geography, everyday objects, academic terms, and general knowledge.

**Answer Guidelines**:
- **Keep answers brief** - Usually 1-3 words
- **Be precise** - Give the exact term asked for
- **Common topics** - Objects, professions, academic terms, natural phenomena, everyday items
