## About Repeat Sentence

Repeat Sentence is a crucial part of the PTE Speaking section where test-takers must listen to a sentence and repeat it exactly as heard. This collection contains 620 real exam sentences.

**Difficulty Levels**:
- **Simple**: Short, straightforward sentences (5-8 words)
- **General**: Medium complexity sentences (9-12 words)
- **Disaster**: Complex, long sentences (13+ words)
