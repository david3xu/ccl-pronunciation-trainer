## Tips for All Answers

### Structure
1. **Opening**: Say what the image is (graph/chart/diagram/table)
2. **Main Content**: Describe key features and data
3. **Closing**: Give a brief conclusion or summary

### Language Tips
- Use simple, easy words (avoid: illustrate, demonstrate, depict)
- Keep sentences short and clear
- Use conversational phrases: "we can see," "there are," "this shows"
- Include specific numbers when available

### Pronunciation
- Avoid complex vocabulary
- Practice smooth transitions between ideas
- Speak at a natural, steady pace
