# PTE Describe Image - Natural Speaking Templates

> **设计原则：** 自然口语，多样化句式，只填名词

---

## Template 1: 极值模板 - 单分类

**适用于:** bar/line/pie chart, table, map

```
This [chart type] shows information about [title].

Looking at the data, the highest number appears in [item], close to [value].

Following that, [item] comes second, with about [value].

The lowest can be found in [item], at only [value].

[Item] and [item] also feature in this chart.

Overall, the data tells us about [title].
```

**Example - Why students study foreign languages:**
```
This pie chart shows information about why students choose to study foreign languages.

Looking at the data, the highest number appears in Personal Interest and enjoyment, close to 43 percent.

Following that, Degree requirement comes second, with about 24 percent.

The lowest can be found in Other, at only 1 percent.

Use in future career and Future travel also feature in this chart.

Overall, the data tells us about why students study foreign languages.
```

## Template 2: 极值模板 - 多分类

**适用于:** grouped bar chart, multi-line graph, dual-axis graph

```
This [chart type] shows information about [title].

Looking at [category 1], the highest number appears in [item], close to [value].

The lowest number can be found in [item], at only [value].

Moving to [category 2], the highest is in [item], at around [value].

[Category 1] and [category 2] are both shown in this chart.

In summary, the data tells us about [title].
```

**Example - Newspaper Circulation USA:**
```
This line graph shows information about Newspaper Circulation in USA.

Looking at Weekday, the highest number appears in 1960, close to 115 percent.

The lowest number can be found in 2017, at only 28 percent.

Moving to Sunday, the highest is in 1960, at around 90 percent.

Weekday and Sunday are both shown in this chart.

In summary, the data tells us about Newspaper Circulation in USA.
```

## Template 3: 流程模板

**适用于:** flow chart, cycle diagram, process

```
This diagram illustrates the process of [title].

It begins with [step 1], which leads to [step 2].

From there, [step 3] takes place.

The process concludes with [final step].

Together, these steps explain how [title] works.
```

**循环图结尾替换:**
```
After [final step], the cycle returns to [step 1].
```

---

## Template 4: 图片模板

**适用于:** photos, illustrations, diagrams without numbers

```
This picture presents information about [topic].

On one side, [element] and [element] are visible.

Moving across, [element] stands out clearly.

[Element] and [element] also appear in the image.

Altogether, the picture illustrates [topic].
```

|----------|----------|

| 流程图/循环图 | Template 3 |
